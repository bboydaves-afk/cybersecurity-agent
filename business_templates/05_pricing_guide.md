## Pricing Factors

### Adjust UP for:
- Urgent timeline (< 2 weeks notice): +25-50%
- Complex environments (hybrid cloud, multi-site): +20-40%
- Compliance requirements (PCI, HIPAA): +15-25%
- After-hours / weekend testing required: +20-30%
- Re-testing beyond complimentary window: $100-200/hour

### Adjust DOWN for:
- Multi-service bundle (2+ services): -10-15%
- Annual retainer commitment: -15-20%
- Referral from existing client: -5-10%
- Non-profit / educational institution: -10-20%
